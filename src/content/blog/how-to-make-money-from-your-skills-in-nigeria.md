You don't always need a new business idea to start making money. Sometimes, what you need is already something you know how to do.



Think about it. Are you the person friends ask to design birthday flyers? Do people come to you for makeup advice, photography, writing, video editing or help with Excel? Perhaps you're good at baking, teaching, coding or organising events.



These may seem like ordinary things to you, but someone else could be willing to pay for them.



The truth is, you can make money from your skills in Nigeria without waiting for the perfect job, a large following or significant startup capital. You need to recognize what you can offer and find a way to turn it into something people can buy.



**\- 1. Identify a skill people are willing to pay for**



Start with what you already know how to do.



Your skill doesn't have to be complicated or unusual. It simply needs to solve a problem, save someone time or make their life easier.



For example:



\- Graphic design: Create flyers, brand identities and social media designs.

\- Writing: Offer copywriting, editing or CV-writing services.

\- Photography: Cover events or create product photographs for businesses.

\- Baking: Take orders for cakes, pastries and small chops.

\- Teaching: Offer lessons, tutorials or practical training.

\- Video editing: Help businesses and creators produce engaging content.



Ask yourself: What do people regularly ask me to help them with? What can I do well enough that someone would pay to have it done?



That could be your starting point.



**\- 2. Turn your skill into something people can buy**



There is more than one way to make money from a skill.



You can offer it as a service, teach others how to do it, or package your knowledge into a digital product.



For instance, a graphic designer can earn from designing for clients, but can also create Canva templates and sell them repeatedly.



A makeup artist can offer makeup services while creating a beginner's makeup guide. A fitness instructor can provide personal training and sell workout plans.



This is where things get interesting. Instead of earning only when you exchange your time for money, you can create products that people can purchase whenever they need them.



**\- 3. Start small and find your first customers**



You don't need thousands of followers to make your first sale.



Start with people within your existing network. Share examples of your work on WhatsApp Status, Instagram, TikTok and other platforms where your potential customers spend time.



Show people what you can do, explain how it helps them and make it easy for them to contact you.



You can also offer an introductory price to attract your first customers, collect feedback and build a portfolio.



The goal is not to look like a big business from day one. It is to get started, learn what people want and improve as you go.



**\-  4. Build something beyond one-off payments**



Getting paid for your skill is a great start. Building a business around it is the next step.



Think beyond individual transactions. Can you create a collection of templates? Package your expertise into an ebook? Develop a short course? Build a community around what you know?



The more you structure your skill into products, services and repeatable offerings, the more opportunities you create to earn.



You don't have to do everything at once. Start with one offer, understand your audience and build from there.
