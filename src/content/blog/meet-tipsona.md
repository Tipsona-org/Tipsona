Running a creator business shouldn't be this hard. Most creators juggle separate tools for their storefront, memberships, brand deals, payments, analytics and contracts. Brand deals start in DMs, and too often end in ghosting. Getting paid safely, especially across borders, is a headache. And your data is scattered, so it's hard to see what actually makes you money.

We're building Tipsona to fix that.

## What Tipsona is

Tipsona gives African creators the tools to build, monetize, finance, discover and grow a digital business, all from one account:

- **One creator identity** across every product.
- **Many ways to earn:** products, fans and brands.
- **Local payments and payouts,** built for Africa.
- **Insights** that show what actually drives your revenue.

## Starting with TipStore

TipStore is the first product to open. It lets you sell digital products from your own storefront, at an address like `tipstore.tipsona.co/yourname`: e-books, guides, templates, presets, audio and video lessons, or a link to a course or community you host somewhere else.

Here's how it works:

1. **Set up your storefront** and upload what you sell.
2. **Share your link** wherever your audience already is.
3. **Get paid.** Buyers check out in one step, with or without an account, and get access the moment their payment is verified. Every purchase is saved in their library.

Your dashboard shows every sale, fee and payout. Once your identity is verified, you withdraw your earnings to your bank.

## What comes next

TipStore launches alongside two more products:

- **Tipper,** which matches you with brands for paid UGC work, with escrow-protected payouts.
- **TipStar,** for memberships, donations and exclusive content for your fans.

After them come TipFix, TipLaunch, TipMarket, TipAds, TipAI and TipInsights: more ways to earn and grow, on the same account. You can read about each one on our [products page](/products/).
