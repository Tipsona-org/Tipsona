Billing

Billing questions.

hello@tipsona.com
Can I change plans later?

Yes. When you upgrade, your subscription is activated and new features unlock immediately. When you downgrade, your current billing period stays active and the change takes effect at renewal.

What happens to my products if I downgrade?

You keep access to every product you already have. If you’re over the new plan’s limit, you can’t publish new products until your total is back within the limit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your subscription stays active until the current billing period ends, then moves to the Free plan. No data is deleted immediately when you cancel.

What if my subscription payment fails?

You’re notified and billing is retried. During the grace period your existing products remain available and your storefront stays online. Some premium features may be restricted.

Is there a fee on each sale?

Yes. Each sale is split into a platform fee, payment gateway charges where they apply, and your earnings. Free has a standard transaction fee; Pro and Business have lower fees. Exact fees are still to be announced.

Which billing periods can I choose?

Monthly or yearly.
