FAQ

Questions, answered.

hello@tipsona.com
What can I sell on TipStore?

Digital products: e-books, PDFs, templates, ZIP files, images, audio, video and other downloadable resources. Physical products aren’t supported.

How do my customers get their files?

Access is granted immediately after the payment is verified. Buyers get a confirmation email with their access link, and every purchase is saved in their library at tipstore.com/library.

How do I get paid?

Payments are processed by Paystack and added to your balance. Once your identity (KYC) and payout account are verified, you can request a withdrawal from your dashboard.

Do I need a website?

No. Every seller automatically gets a storefront at tipstore.com/yourname that works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Can people buy without an account?

Yes. Guest checkout only needs an email address, and the purchase is linked to that email.

How are my files protected?

Downloads never expose where your files are stored. Buyers download through signed links that expire after a short time, and every download is tracked.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes your storefront, product uploads and digital product sales, with limited products and storage. Pro and Business add more.

Can I use my own domain?

Custom domains are planned for a future release. For now, your storefront lives at tipstore.com/yourname.
