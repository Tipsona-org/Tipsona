Why Tipsona

Africa does not need another creator app. It needs creator infrastructure.

Tipsona is building a Creator Business Ecosystem: a connected operating layer that brings creator commerce, memberships, UGC brand work, crowdfunding, discovery, advertising, AI, analytics and everyday business utilities into one platform.

  • One creator identity.
  • One ecosystem.
  • Multiple monetization rails.
  • Localized African infrastructure.
  • A compounding network of creators, brands, content, transactions and intelligence.

The problem

The creator journey is fragmented.

Creators, brands and buyers each work around the gaps with separate tools, DMs and workarounds.

  1. 01

    Creators stitch together separate tools for storefronts, memberships, brand deals, payments, analytics and contracts.

  2. 02

    Brands struggle to discover, vet, brief and manage creators at scale.

  3. 03

    Trust, logistics, escrow and cross-border payouts remain fragmented.

  4. 04

    Many African creators have audiences but limited access to direct platform monetization.

  5. 05

    Data is scattered, making it difficult to understand what actually drives revenue.
