Why it matters

The problems are real.

What recent reporting on Nigeria’s creator and digital economy keeps pointing to.

Platform monetization gaps

Direct platform monetization remains limited or inconsistent for many creators, increasing reliance on brand deals and direct-to-fan income.

Income instability

Low average creator earnings, and barriers around monetization infrastructure, capital and operating costs.

Contracts and rights

Unclear deliverables, compensation, usage rights, consent and documentation come up again and again.

IP awareness

Limited awareness of intellectual-property rights is an ongoing challenge.

Online transaction trust

Online purchase fraud is a concern, and escrow is increasingly used to reduce buyer and seller risk.

Operating costs

Internet, data and electricity remain practical constraints for digital creators.

Sources: LaunchPad Nigeria, The Guardian, TechManly, PUNCH, Chambers Global Practice Guides, TechCabal, Marketplace Naija (all 2026) and the Nigerian Copyright Commission.
