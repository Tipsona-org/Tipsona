Brand and design system

How Tipsona looks and moves.

The rules behind the website: logo, colour, contrast, type, the six tip shapes, icons, components and motion. Every Tipsona page is built from these tokens.

Logo

Flame, always, on light and dark surfaces. On a flame surface, use white. Keep clear space equal to the height of the “T” bar on every side, and never set it smaller than 20px tall.

Colour

Flame leads and the logo is always flame. Night, a warm near-black, carries dark sections. Four illustration colours give each tip shape its own voice. Neutrals lean warm.

  • Text meets WCAG AA

    Body text is 4.5:1 or better against its background. Large display text (24px bold and up) is 3:1 or better.

  • Flame is a surface, not a text colour

    Flame on light backgrounds is only 2.9:1. Use it for the logo, buttons, highlights and shapes. Text on flame is white and large: 19px bold or bigger. For orange words on light, use Ember.

  • Pair every surface with its text token

    Canvas and paper take ink. Night takes cream. Flame takes white at large sizes. Sun, lilac, blush and lime take ink. Never place muted text on tinted illustration colours.

  • Status is never colour alone

    Pills pair colour with a word and a dot: Paid, Pending, Failed. Charts pair colour with labels.

How it works

Data · Fragment Mono
13px · tabular figures

Order #TPS-24817 · ₦7,500.00 · GH₵ 450.00

  • One loud thing per screen

    Each section has one display headline and at most one flame element. Everything around it stays quiet.

  • Every section opens with a file tab

    A mono tab label names the section, then the headline, then one short lede. The tab carries a tip shape that matches the topic.

  • Size, then weight, then colour

    Hierarchy comes from the type scale first. Colour is the last resort, not the first.

  • Numbers are content

    Prices, balances and order IDs use Inter Tight or Fragment Mono with tabular figures, so they line up and read like receipts.

Tip shapes

Six shapes, each with one meaning and one colour. They mark navigation, fill icons, build product covers, float in heroes and become stickers. Use the shape that matches what you are talking about.

A personcircle · flame

Creators, buyers, avatars. The most human shape.

A tip or ideastar · sun

Knowledge, highlights, “how it works”.

A productsquare · lilac

Files, e-books, templates, anything for sale.

A storefrontarch · night

Stores, doorways, photo frames.

Moneypill · mint

Prices, payments, payouts, balances.

Trustdiamond · blush

Security, verification, protection.

Icons

Motion

Four curves do all the work. Press a card to play it. Scroll-linked scenes pin only on large screens, loops pause when off screen, and everything stops for people who ask for reduced motion.

UX rules

  • Write from the seller’s side

    Say “Withdraw to your bank”, not “Initiate payout”. Buttons say exactly what happens: “Claim your link”, “Pay ₦7,500”.

  • Show real money

    Always show currency symbols (₦, GH₵) and full amounts. Fees are shown before they are taken.

  • Validate as people type

    Usernames are lowercased and cleaned as you type, with a clear reason when a name is not available and a suggestion to try.

  • Motion explains, never blocks

    Animation shows cause and effect: a payment lands, a file flies to the library. Content is readable without it, and reduced-motion settings turn it off.

  • Thumb-first

    Touch targets are at least 44px. Primary actions are full-width on phones. Nothing scrolls sideways except data tables.

  • Fast by default

    Self-hosted, subsetted fonts. WebP images with fixed sizes and lazy loading. Animation libraries load deferred and pause when off screen.
