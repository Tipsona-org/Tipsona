Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect straight away and unlock new features immediately. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.

What happens to my products if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. You keep every product you’ve published, but you can’t publish new ones until you’re within your new plan’s limit.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the billing period, then moves to Free. Your storefront and products stay online.

What if my subscription payment fails?

We’ll tell you and try again. During a short grace period your storefront stays online and buyers can keep purchasing while you update your card.

Do you take a fee on each sale?

Yes, a small platform fee plus payment processing, shown on every order. Pro and Business plans pay a lower fee.

Which billing periods can I choose?

Monthly or yearly. Paying yearly gives you two months free.
