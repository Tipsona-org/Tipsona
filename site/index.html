FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Write to us and a real person will reply.

hello@tipsona.com
What can I sell on Tipsona?

Anything digital a buyer can download: e-books, PDFs, templates, ZIP files, images, audio and video. Physical products aren’t supported.

How do my customers receive their files?

As soon as a payment is confirmed, buyers get instant access on the confirmation page, an email with their download link and a permanent copy in their Tipsona library. You never send a file by hand.

How and when do I get paid?

Payments are processed by Paystack and added to your Tipsona balance. Once your identity and bank account are verified, you can request a withdrawal from your dashboard at any time.

Do I need a website or technical skills?

No. Your storefront lives at tipsona.com/@yourname and works on every phone. If you can post on Instagram, you can set up Tipsona.

Can people buy without creating an account?

Yes. Guest checkout only asks for a name and email address. Buyers can create an account later to keep all their purchases in one library.

How do you stop people from sharing my files?

Downloads never reveal where your file is stored. Each buyer gets a private link that expires after a few minutes, and a fresh one is created when they return to their library.

Is Tipsona free to use?

Yes. The Free plan lets you publish up to 10 products with no monthly fee. Upgrade when you want unlimited products, lower fees or advanced analytics.

Can I use my own domain name?

Custom domains are coming soon for Pro and Business plans. Until then, your storefront lives at tipsona.com/@yourname.
